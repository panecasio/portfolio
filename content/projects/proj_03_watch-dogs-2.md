As a **Level Designer** on *Watch Dogs 2*, I worked on the main campaign mission entitled *"Man Versus Machine"*.

> {{< youtube _zAjQk63fCA >}}
> *A brief overview where I narrate a level design breakdown of the mission.*

## Overview

This mission takes place in the TIDIS Corporate HQ in the Silicon Valley district. I worked with level artists [Jonathan Wiley]("https://cenelder.artstation.com/") and [Sarah Cole-Megaro]("http://sarahcolemegaro.com/?page_id=1741") to design all interior and exterior spaces, starting from the initial whitebox geometry of the building campus and then worked inwards, covering mission interiors as we developed the pitch for the mission.

The mission has 3 major beats; the starting and end sections of this mission have the player taking control of 2 (two) exotic robots (Wrench Junior and The Spider Drone) that are exclusive to this mission. I worked with game designer [Michael Surya]("https://www.linkedin.com/in/michael-surya-05b4b328") and gameplay programmer [Sergio Juarez]("https://ca.linkedin.com/in/sergeminator") to pitch and iterate on the design for both of these robots.
 
## Exotic Beats: Wrench Junior, A VR Puzzle, and The Spider Drone

> ![A screenshot of Watch Dogs 2's "Man Versus Machine" mission. The player is remotely controlling the Wrench Junior robot.](/img/projects/watch_dogs_2/wd2_wrench_junior_01.png)
>*The player is remotely controlling the Wrench Junior robot in the first of three beats in the mission. The challenge is to move Wrench Junior through an automated repair facility by hacking its cameras and re-routing its conveyor belt system. Wrench Junior is then used to detonate an EMP that brings down the security and forces an evacuation at TIDIS HQ so that Marcus can sneak in on foot.*

>![A screenshot of Watch Dogs 2's "Man Versus Machine" mission. The player is solving a Virtual Reality Puzzle as Marcus.](/img/projects/watch_dogs_2/wd2_marcus_02.png)
>*In the middle section of the mission, the Player is back to controlling Marcus. After downloading a trove of top secret files, Marcus wants to push deeper underground at TIDIS. To do this, Players have to crack a mind-bending VR Puzzle that's baked into the building's geometry. I had **so much fun** designing this puzzle and consider it some of my best work!*

>![A screenshot of Watch Dogs 2's "Man Versus Machine" mission. The player is looking at a giant Spider Drone.](/img/projects/watch_dogs_2/wd2_spider_01.png)
>*Having solved the VR Puzzle, Marcus enters the hangar of the Spider Drone. Time to hijack it to start the third and final act of the mission!*

>![A screenshot of Watch Dogs 2's "Man Versus Machine" mission. The player is remotely controlling the Spider Drone.](/img/projects/watch_dogs_2/wd2_spider_02.png)
>*We really wanted players to have fun in this final beat so much of the gameplay here focuses on climbing on the walls and ceilings, blasting and / or stomping anything in the Spider Drone's path. We tweaked the NPC damage profiles here so they do very little damage!*
